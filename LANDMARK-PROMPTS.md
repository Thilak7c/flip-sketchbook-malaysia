# Malaysia Sketchbook — Ready-to-Generate Watercolor Prompts

These prompts are designed as one consistent visual series for an interactive open-sketchbook website. The shared requirements are already included in every prompt: landscape open-book composition, visible center gutter, warm limestone paper, delicate ink, translucent watercolor, and no readable text.

Two naming assumptions are used here: **Blue Mosque** means the Sultan Salahuddin Abdul Aziz Shah Mosque in Shah Alam, Selangor; **Penang’s Pagoda** means the Kek Lok Si Pagoda, also known as the Pagoda of Ten Thousand Buddhas.

## 1. Saloma Link, Kuala Lumpur

```text
Create a high-resolution editorial watercolor illustration for a Malaysian travel sketchbook.

Subject: Saloma Link pedestrian bridge in Kuala Lumpur at blue hour after a light tropical rain. Show the bridge’s distinctive woven diamond lattice and illuminated geometric structure crossing above the Klang River, with a restrained Kuala Lumpur skyline and warm city lights softened by humid evening haze. The bridge must remain recognizable and architecturally coherent, with the lattice forming the main visual rhythm.

Composition: an open landscape sketchbook spread with two facing pages, a subtle vertical center gutter, softly rounded paper edges, and generous warm limestone paper margins. Use a slightly low viewpoint so the bridge creates a graceful diagonal across the spread. Keep the center gutter clear of the bridge’s most important structural details.

Art direction: delicate charcoal-brown ink linework with translucent watercolor washes, muted indigo, smoky blue-gray, palm green, warm ochre, faded terracotta, and a very restrained hibiscus-vermilion accent. Include visible paper fibers, imperfect pigment blooms, soft dry-brush edges, and quiet editorial travel-journal atmosphere. The lights should appear as gentle watercolor glows, never neon.

No readable words, logos, watermarks, UI, photorealism, fantasy architecture, excessive traffic, crowded people, oversaturated neon, or distorted bridge geometry. Landscape 16:9, no text.
```
